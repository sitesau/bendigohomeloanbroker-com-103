Costs and accreditation

One of the common questions borrowers ask is about the cost of using a broker. In most cases, a mortgage broker Bendigo borrowers engage does not charge a fee to the borrower. Brokers are typically paid a commission by the lender rather than by you. Under ASIC rules, any exceptions to this must be disclosed upfront. It is also important to consider the qualifications and protections in place. Brokers in the network usually hold an Australian Credit Licence or operate as Credit Representatives. They are commonly accredited with industry bodies such as the MFAA or FBAA. Since 1 January 2021, Australian mortgage brokers have a legal obligation to act in the consumer's best interests. This means they must prioritise your needs when providing credit assistance. If issues arise, network partners are members of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), providing access to free external dispute resolution. These safeguards ensure that the advice you receive is professional and that there is a pathway to resolve any complaints.

Loan types and features

Different borrowers have different needs, and a good broker will tailor the loan structure accordingly. For first home buyers, the focus is often on securing a loan with a manageable deposit. Eligible first home buyers may be able to purchase with as little as a 5 percent deposit without Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) under the federal guarantee. For refinancing, the goal is often to secure a better interest rate or consolidate debt. Investors may prefer interest-only loans to manage cash flow, or principal and interest loans to build equity faster. A broker will compare variable and fixed rate options and look at features like offset accounts to see what fits your situation best. A refinancer may care more about consolidation or future access to equity than a marginal change in rate. An investor may care more about structure and portfolio fit than a single feature. A buyer seeking pre approval may care most about whether the lender can move cleanly from conditional approval to formal approval.
